Node operation and validator admission.

The original network launched with one operator. Historical documentation describes restrictions on new validator deposits. Development work alone does not establish that admission is enabled on the deployed network.

Verify the active release.

Before bonding, verify the deployed release, funded deposit support, admission configuration, and checkpoint requirements. Use the release’s operator guide for actual commands and network identifiers.

The published genesis-verification window ended on 5 September 2026. A new operator must check the current checkpoint and synchronization procedure rather than follow an expired onboarding deadline.

The published RPC endpoint.

The existing wallet connects through the Postern Labs endpoint below. This website does not sign transactions or send RPC requests.

https://posternlabs.com/g4rpc

Endpoint behavior, availability, and enabled methods must be checked against the active network release.

Size your node for its actual work.

Historical resource measurements were taken from a young chain and a concentrated operator fleet. They do not establish current storage, replay, bandwidth, or independent-validator requirements.

Consult the active operator guide, monitor replay and peering, and account for chain growth. A validator also accepts operational and slashing exposure.
