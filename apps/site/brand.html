Bloch Protocol

White ground, one accent spent deliberately, and a serif that signals a project which publishes specifications rather than slogans.

The mark

A sphere, an equator, a state vector.

The Bloch sphere is the geometric picture of a single qubit — every possible state on one surface. The mark is that picture reduced to three strokes. It is drawn as inline SVG in protocol colors, so it inherits light and dark themes without a second asset.

The mark at display size.

Rules

Three, only

Keep the vector pointing up-right — it is a state, not a clock hand. Do not fill the sphere. Do not add a wordmark inside the circle; the name sits beside it, in the display serif.

Typography

Three voices, zero webfonts.

Voice

The rule that writes every page.

Do

State the cost

Every claimed benefit ships with its trade-off attached. A protocol page that only lists benefits is marketing.

Do

Label every figure

Measured and labelled as such, or planned and labelled as such. A number with no label is a rumor with typography.

Don’t

Promise dates or prices

No launch dates, no price talk, no “soon”. Nothing on this site is investment advice, and the copy never forgets it.
