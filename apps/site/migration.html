The sequence

Four phases, in order.

Each one only begins when the one above it has finished.

  1. now

    Proof of work, running live

    The chain produces blocks under SHA-256d with merged mining. Nothing about it changes until height 50,000: transactions confirm, the explorer indexes, miners are paid.

  2. height 50,000

    The chain halts days away

    Blocks above 50,000 are invalid — a consensus rule compiled into every node, not a switch anyone throws. A chain does not stop because it was announced; if late blocks were merely unwanted, miners would keep producing them and the “halt” would be a fork nobody agreed to. Making them invalid is what actually ends the chain.

    Mining revenue ends here. A signed snapshot of every balance is taken at this height.

  3. the gap

    About six months with no chain

    Balances sit in the snapshot; the explorer keeps serving history. This is the plan, not a failure — the time is for review before a chain restarts with different consensus.

    One consequence stated plainly: once mining stops, the old chain’s history stops being evidence — proof-of-work security is bought with ongoing hashrate. That is why the canonical artifact is the signed snapshot, whose digest goes into the Genesis-4 genesis block, and not whatever chain has the most work afterwards.

  4. genesis-4

    Proof of stake, from the snapshot

    Every Genesis-3 balance crosses as an ordinary liquid balance — 448,337 UTXOs, carried automatically. Carried-over coins can be staked and delegated like any other coin.

Refuse these

There is no migration action. None.

The carryover is automatic, which makes every “migration step” someone offers you a theft attempt by definition.

Scam shape 1

“Claim your Genesis-4 tokens”

There is no claim to file. Balances carry across in the snapshot without any action from you.

Scam shape 2

“Send coins to the bridge”

There is no bridge, no contract to interact with, nobody to send anything to. Anyone asking you to move tokens to migrate them is stealing them.

Scam shape 3

“Launch is on this date”

There is no Genesis-4 launch date. The gap is measured in months and ends when review does — anyone quoting a date is guessing or baiting.
