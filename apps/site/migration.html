From proof of work to proof of stake.

  1. Genesis-3 stopped. Blocks above height 39,918 are invalid under its terminal-height rule.
  2. A snapshot preserved the ledger. The published record reports byte-identical outputs produced independently on two nodes.
  3. Genesis-4 opened. The opening ledger included the carryover and genesis allocations.

Your balance did not need a claim.

No token migration payment.

The carryover happened automatically. It did not require a claim, a swap, a bridge transfer, or disclosure of a recovery phrase.

Genesis-3 mining ended with that chain. Genesis-4 uses proof of stake. The old proof-of-work history remains a historical record.
