02 / A SINGLE WORKFLOW

Every movement has a reason, a signer and a result.

Keep operational intent, network execution and accounting evidence connected.

01 / PREPARE

Define the request

Bind an invoice, payout or treasury instruction to an amount, destination, reference and network identity.

02 / APPROVE

Apply the policy

Evaluate roles, recipient rules and limits before a designated device signs the transaction.

03 / TRACK

Follow the network

Distinguish submitted, included, confirmed and qualified-finality states rather than collapsing them into “paid.”

04 / RECONCILE

Close the loop

Match transaction outputs and status to the originating instruction; preserve exceptions and an audit export.

All four steps describe the target product workflow. They depend on a hardened wallet, complete indexing and verified status data.

03 / CONTROLS + EVIDENCE

Designed for responsible operations.

Make decisions reviewable and payment states explicit before expanding to partner systems.

01Authorization by policy

Separate preparation, approval and signing; record the rule version and decision for each transfer.

02Device-held keys

Use native wallet signing without assuming an initial Bloch Pay custody role.

03Finality-aware receipts

Link a payment reference to transaction inputs, outputs, amounts, height, slot and a qualified status.

04Reconciliation by design

Compare business instructions, network records and internal books with visible exception handling.
