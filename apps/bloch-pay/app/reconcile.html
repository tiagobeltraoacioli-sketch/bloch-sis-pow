ONE BATCH · ONE CONFIRMATION

Resolve exceptions before import

  1. Match referencesRecords match exact invoice references, ignoring letter case. No fuzzy allocation.
  2. Inspect differencesIdentical records are skipped. Conflicting references, reused outputs and invalid rows block the entire batch.
  3. Confirm the impactReview partial matches and excess amounts. New records are appended together; existing invoices and records stay intact.
CSV column guide

reference, invoice_reference, amount_BLCH, record_date are required. Dates use YYYY-MM-DD. Optional: txid, block_id, output_index, note. Provide all three transaction fields together. Optional direction must match the invoice. An imported evidence label never establishes verification.
