A role for every participant

PARTNER MODEL
FIAT ACCESS

Banks & regulated PSPs

Account access, payment initiation, domestic rail connectivity, settlement reporting and beneficiary credit.

DIGITAL-ASSET ACCESS

VASPs & PSAVs

Partner-operated on/off ramps, virtual-asset transfers and conversions, with identity and transfer-information responsibilities mapped per corridor.

ASSET OPERATIONS

Custodians

Safekeeping, controlled asset release and custody evidence where the selected partner model requires them.

CONVERSION + FUNDING

FX & liquidity providers

Partner quotes, fees, liquidity reservations and funding. Fiat and digital-asset legs keep their own amounts and settlement records.

BUSINESS CONNECTIONS

Platforms & operators

ERP, marketplaces, payment orchestrators and treasury teams connect business references to the underlying partner instructions.

OBSERVATION + REVIEW

Data & risk services

Evidence, exception review and optional Red & Blue integration. Participant authorization and transaction decisions remain with the responsible institution.

04 / SHARED INTEGRATION CONTRACT

Instructions in. Evidence out.

Download API design ↓

Partner adapter contract

Payment intents carry exact minor-unit amounts, source and destination currencies, participant references, and an explicit conversion quote reference when provided.

  • Idempotent instruction creation
  • Separate funding and payout legs
  • Partner-scoped identities and permissions
  • Signed event and settlement references
  • Return handling and reconciliation
The downloadable OpenAPI contract is a design artifact; no managed API endpoint is deployed.

Qualification before connection

Each corridor needs a responsible partner and a tested operating model.

  • Entity eligibility, licensing scope and rail access
  • Customer identity, screening and transfer information
  • FX, liquidity, fees and funding arrangements
  • Custody or safeguarding responsibilities
  • Authenticated adapters and replay-resistant events
  • Settlement evidence, exception handling and recovery
