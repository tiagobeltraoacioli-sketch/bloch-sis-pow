## 2. Findings (ordered by severity)

### FC-01 — Post-e1400, ~60 epochs without any included attestation leaks every validator to zero and the chain dies permanently (no proposer can ever be drawn)

- **Severity:** High (permanent, un-forkable-without-hard-fork liveness loss; precondition is a fleet-wide outage or fleet-wide attestation-inclusion failure of ≈15–18 h, which is a realistic operational event for a 64-validator / 5-host fleet).
- **Status:** NEW. The closest prior text is `params.rs:104-113` (`MAX_EPOCH_ADVANCE`), which claims the network "may be dark and still resume on the same chain" for **45.5 days**; that claim is false since epoch 1400.
- **Files:** `finality.rs:518-556` (leak bite), `transition.rs:5316-5333` (`with_leak_applied`, saturating to 0), `transition.rs:2569-2576` (`consensus_roster_at`), `transition.rs:5539-5541` (boundary roll), `transition.rs:5570-5573` (proposer check), `sample.rs:76` (`effective_stake > 0` filter), `schedule.rs:156-158` (`proposer` → `None`), `engine.rs:1810-1830` (`propose` returns when `proposer != Some(index)`), `finality.rs:603` (post-2700 recovery requires a *valid vote*).
- **Description.** The bite is `remaining · t / 64` with `t = since_finality − 4` (`finality.rs:542`); at `t = 64` it equals `remaining`, and integer rounding takes a 1.4e16-sat bond to 0 already around `t ≈ 55`. `close_epoch` is run for *every skipped epoch* when the next block arrives (`while st.epoch < block_epoch { close_epoch }`, :5539), and an epoch with no block has no `pending_votes`, so **every** validator is "absent" and leaks. Once `LEAKED_ROSTER_ACTIVATION_EPOCH` binds (it has, at 1400), `consensus_roster_at` subtracts the leak from every weight; when all weights are 0, `sample` filters everything out, `schedule::proposer` returns `None`, step 4 rejects every block as `NotScheduledProposer`, and the node's own `propose` never builds (it evaluates the same draw off `rolled_to`). Post-2700 recovery cannot fire: it only credits validators with a `valid` vote (:603), and a vote needs a block. The floor (`MIN_QUORUM_DENOMINATOR`) is on the *denominator*, not on the roster, so it does not help.
- **Scenario.** Last finalized epoch F. Fleet dark (cloud incident, bad rollout, mesh collapse, or a release that silently breaks attestation inclusion) from epoch F+2. First node back at wall epoch ≥ F+60: `rolled_to` rolls ~58 boundaries, the leak zeroes all 64, no proposer exists, nobody proposes, no block can ever be valid. There is no protocol path out; only a coordinated binary change (reset the ledger or re-genesis). Below the threshold (say a 30-epoch outage) the chain resumes with everybody carrying a proportional leak, which post-2700 drains in ~8 epochs — so the cliff is sharp and unannounced.
- **Evidence.** `finality.rs:542`: `let bite = ((remaining as u128 * t) / INACTIVITY_LEAK_QUOTIENT).max(1) as u64; let bite = bite.min(remaining);` — `sample.rs:76`: `validators.iter().filter(|v| v.effective_stake > 0)` — `schedule.rs:157`: `sample(.., 1).first().copied()`.
- **Recommendation.** (a) Bound the bite so the roster weight can never reach zero for *everyone* (e.g. cap `t` at 63, or floor the leak-adjusted roster weight at a fraction of the duty weight, mirroring the denominator floor); or (b) make the proposer draw fall back to the unleaked `duty_roster_at` when the leak-adjusted total is 0. Either is a flag day. (c) Fix the `MAX_EPOCH_ADVANCE` doc: the real dark-time ceiling is the leak, not 4,096 epochs. (d) Add a test that rolls `close_epoch` across ≥ 70 empty epochs and asserts a proposer still exists. Confidence: **high** (pure code path; every step read).

### FC-02 — Genesis-cohort cap hands a supermajority to the first minimum-bond independent validator once the taper is deep

- **Severity:** High with precondition (funded admission must be armed at epoch L — planned for Oct 2026 — and the taper must have progressed: >1/3 for one outsider at bps < 6667 ≈ 2027-02, 2/3 at the floor 2027-08-13).
- **Status:** KNOWN mechanism (TM2 G2: "the cohort's allowed weight is pinned to a multiple of whatever outsiders happen to hold"); the G2 *halt* was closed by the `Deferred` arm, but the **inversion** — a single 25,000-BLCH outsider ending with a finality supermajority and 2/3 of proposer draws — is not recorded anywhere I could find (`grep -ri "CAP_MEANINGFUL\|first independent" docs/` → nothing). NEW as a consequence.
- **Files:** `genesis_cohort.rs:108` (`CAP_MEANINGFUL_AT_SAT = MIN_DEPOSIT_SAT`), `:111-127` (`cap_status`), `:176-194` (`cap = others · bps/(10000−bps)`, pro-rata scale), `transition.rs:2532` (applied inside `duty_roster_at`, hence in the quorum denominator, the proposer draw and the partition weights).
- **Description.** The cap is a share of *post-cap* stake, solved as `cohort ≤ O · s/(1−s)` where `O` is total non-cohort stake. At the floor `s = 1/3` that is `cohort ≤ O/2`. If exactly one outsider holds `O = 25,000 BLCH`, all 64 cohort validators are scaled to 12,500 BLCH *combined* and the outsider holds 2/3 of `total_active`: it satisfies `3w ≥ 2·total` alone (`finality.rs:478`), so it justifies and finalizes any checkpoint it likes on its own branch, is drawn as proposer 2/3 of the time, and dominates every slot committee's LMD-GHOST weight. Two colluding outsiders reach the same at any `s ≤ 1/2`. The rule that was meant to say "the founder cannot halt the chain alone" also says "whoever posts the first bond can *finalize* alone".
- **Evidence.** `genesis_cohort.rs:126`: `CapStatus::Enforced { cap_sat: others * bps / (10_000 - bps) }`; `:108`: `pub const CAP_MEANINGFUL_AT_SAT: u128 = crate::staking::MIN_DEPOSIT_SAT;`.
- **Recommendation.** Do not let *absolute* cohort weight be a function of outsider stake alone: cap the cohort *share* only once outsider stake exceeds a meaningful fraction of the cohort (e.g. defer while `others < cohort_stake · s/(1−s) · k` for a chosen k), or keep a cohort weight floor equal to a fixed fraction of its bonded stake. Founder decision; needs a flag day before L. Add a test: one 25k outsider at `epoch ≥ COHORT_TAPER_EPOCHS` must not reach `is_supermajority` alone. Confidence: **high** on the arithmetic; the exploit precondition is the roadmap, not a bug.

- **Files:** `transition.rs:3489-3527` (gate falls through at `u64::MAX`; no verifier; `exit_epoch = epoch + 32`), `transition.rs:2483-2490` (`duty_roster_at` drops `epoch >= exit_epoch`), `engine.rs:5844` (mempool-only refusal).

### FC-04 — RANDAO grinding of the *next* epoch's committees and proposer schedule is free and one epoch too close (F6 open, proposer reward absent)

- **Severity:** Medium (needs a validator that proposes tail slots; on today's founder-run fleet the actor is the founder, but it is a live protocol weakness and compounds).
- **Status:** KNOWN in two halves, both gated inert: `ANCESTRY_SEED_ACTIVATION_EPOCH = u64::MAX` (`params.rs:1182`, `transition.rs:2354-2400`: `back = 1`, seed for E is the close of E−1) and `REWARDS_V2_ACTIVATION_EPOCH = u64::MAX` rule 4 (`params.rs:1647-1660`: "there is no separate proposer reward to forfeit"). TM2 G3. What is NEW is stating the combination: the price the spec §6.3 and `beacon.rs:52` put on withholding ("forfeited proposer reward") is **zero** on this chain, and the target of the grind is the immediately following epoch.
- **Description.** The proposer of the last `t` slots of epoch E−1 sees, before publishing, the full epoch-E partition and proposer schedule for each of `2^t` reveal/withhold choices, picks the one giving it more tail slots in E (and hence more bits for E+1), at no cost: issuance is pro-rata to stake with attestation credit only (`tests/committee.rs:797 rewards_are_pro_rata_to_stake_not_to_block_production`), fees are negligible. Fork-choice weight per slot is ~2 validators, so concentrating one's validators into one slot's committee also buys intra-epoch reorg leverage.
- **Doc/test divergence:** `committees.rs:50-52` module header and `schedule.rs:47-52` state the look-ahead as *the* rule; `tests/committee.rs:1309` (`withholding_in_the_tail_of_an_epoch_cannot_resort_the_next_epochs_partition`) proves the property for `seeded_epoch_committees`, a function the live transition does not use below the gate — the test gives assurance about an inert rule. The spec (§6.3, Appendix A) has no look-ahead constant at all.
- **Recommendation.** Arm `ANCESTRY_SEED` (its own docs list the replay precondition) and price withholding (REWARDS_V2 rule 4) in the same flag-day plan; retitle/augment the committee test so it also asserts what `CommittedState::seed_for_epoch` actually does today. Confidence: **high**.

### FC-05 — LMD-GHOST has no proposer boost and a root-value tie-break: ex-ante reorgs and balancing are cheap at 2 attesters per slot

- **Severity:** Medium (reorg/censorship lever; requires ≥ a slot committee's worth of colluding validators plus a proposer slot; no safety break by itself).
- **Status:** KNOWN as an unimplemented recommendation (`docs/specs/BLOCH-POS-THREAT-MODEL.md:53`, `docs/whitepaper/ED2-CRYPTO-SECURITY.md:491`: "late attestation inclusion, proposer boost — recommendations, not yet"). The concrete shape below is not written anywhere.
- **Files:** `forkchoice.rs:223-262` (`head`, tie-break `w == best_w && *child > best`), `engine.rs:1734-1772` (`attest` at slot start, `head = head_id()`), `engine.rs:5212-5217` (attest at t=0, propose at t=slot/3).
- **Description.** With 64 validators and 32 slots each slot committee has 2 members, so a block's whole intra-slot weight is ≤ 2/64 of stake. Ex-ante: an adversary holding both members of slot s's committee and the proposer of slot s+1 withholds slot-s votes, builds s+1 on s−1; block s and block s+1' tie at zero weight; the tie goes to the lexicographically larger root, which the adversary grinds by editing its body (`body_root` → header → id). Honest attesters at s+1 then vote for the winner. Balancing: two competing branches kept within one vote of each other by releasing withheld votes just in time keeps honest votes split indefinitely (finality still needs 2/3 of *target* votes, which the split does not prevent, so this is liveness/reorg, not safety). Ties are also a *proposer-controlled* outcome (root grinding) rather than a random one.
- **Recommendation.** Add proposer boost (weight the current slot's timely block by a fraction of the committee weight) or at minimum make the tie-break non-grindable (e.g. lower root, or prefer the earlier slot). Consensus change → flag day. Confidence: **medium-high** (attack shape reasoned from code; not simulated).

### FC-06 — Node-side fork choice is order- and store-shape-dependent (O01), and feeds *every stored block* including orphans

- **Severity:** Medium (two honest nodes with the same messages can bar different equivocators and pick different heads; node-local, not committed; no safety break).
- **Status:** KNOWN (external audit O01, `forkchoice.rs:11-38`, `params.rs:1680-1733`; fix gated inert). NEW detail: `engine.rs:5450-5470` iterates **all** of `self.blocks` (canonical and orphaned) in root order, then the pool in `(validator, root)` order, so whether a validator is barred depends on which orphans a node happens to have stored — a purely local artefact of sync history.
- **Recommendation.** Arm `FORKCHOICE_EQUIVOCATION_HORIZON` for the committed fold; in the node, build the store from the committed `latest_messages`/`fc_equivocators` of the head state plus the pool, not from a scan of every stored envelope. Confidence: **high** on the mechanism.

### FC-07 — With the 1/2 floor armed, any two disjoint sets holding ≥ 1/3 of unleaked stake each finalize conflicting checkpoints after ~25 epochs, with no slashable offence by anyone

- **Severity:** Medium (documented residual; needs a ≥ 1/3 partition or a ≥ 1/3 adversary willing to run a censoring fork for ~7 h).
- **Status:** KNOWN — post-mortem §5 ("bounds the divergence to at most three ways"), `params.rs:236-262`, `finality.rs:60-85` (module header now corrected), `finality::tests::the_quorum_floor_is_the_one_the_owner_chose`. Verified: `total_active = max(leak_adjusted, unleaked/2)` (`finality.rs:380-403`); for present fraction p after full leak, `3p ≥ 2·max(p,1/2)` ⇔ `p ≥ 1/3`; the honest majority finalizes normally, the minority fork's proposers omit majority votes so the majority leaks on *that* fold; neither side double-votes or surrounds, so accountable safety has nothing to slash — and `SLASHING_EVIDENCE` is inert regardless. Ethereum has the same shape without the floor; the floor is the founder's accepted trade. Nothing to add beyond: integrators must keep following SECURITY.md's "two independent nodes + 30 uninterrupted epochs" rule.

### FC-08 — Sub-epoch duty-view lag: with `back = 1` the seed and the source checkpoint for epoch E depend on the *last block* of E−1, which honest first-slot attesters may not have seen

- **Severity:** Medium-Low (participation/reward loss for the first slots of every epoch whenever the last block is late; an adversarial tail proposer can *choose* to release late and knock out the first-slot committee's votes and credit; during a stall those honest validators then leak).
- **Status:** KNOWN in part — `params.rs:455-460` ("the sub-epoch-lag case of the duty-view ANCHOR defect… a mitigation, not a fix"). `engine.rs:1734-1772`: `attest` derives committee and `source` from `rolled_to(e)` of the node's own head at slot start; `seed_for_epoch` reads `boundary_mixes[E−1]`, i.e. the mix *after* the last block of E−1. If that block arrives after the attester computed its duty, the attestation lands in the wrong slot committee (rejected at step 8) or carries a stale `source` (dropped at the tally). `seed_for_attestation` (`engine.rs:1498`) correctly anchors *judging* to the target branch, but *producing* cannot be anchored to a block the node has not received.
- **Recommendation.** Arming `ANCESTRY_SEED` fixes the seed half (E−2 is closed before E−1 starts); the source half is inherent to FFG and is what late-inclusion (previous-epoch attestations in the next epoch, as Ethereum allows) would fix — today `epoch_of(att.slot) == st.epoch` (`transition.rs:5751`) forbids it. Confidence: **high**.

### FC-09 — `close_epoch` silently swallows `FinalityError::OutOfOrderEpoch`; a desynchronised engine would stop finality forever with no signal

- **Severity:** Low (unreachable on mainnet today: genesis pins `epoch = 0` and the engine at checkpoint 0 (`transition.rs:2192-2196`); `ws::anchor` has no node caller).
- **Status:** NEW.
- **Files:** `transition.rs:4925-4928` (`let _ = st.finality_engine.process_epoch(&epoch_votes);`), `finality.rs:329-334` (`next_epoch` only advances on success).
- **Description.** If `finality_engine.next_epoch != closing` once (a devnet genesis at a non-zero epoch, a future WS-checkpoint boot whose checkpoint epoch ≠ state epoch − 1, or any refactor that constructs `FinalityState` separately from `epoch`), *every* subsequent epoch is rejected, `justified`/`finalized` freeze, no error is logged, and the state root still commits the frozen fold so all nodes agree on a dead finality. The leak still runs (it is inside the same call, which never runs) — actually it does not run either, so the chain would also never leak, i.e. never recover.
- **Recommendation.** Turn the `Err` into a `consensus_invariant!` (the condition is internal — `closing` and `next_epoch` are both committed state, never attacker-supplied) or, better, drop `next_epoch` and pass `closing` as the authority. Add a test constructing a state whose engine is one epoch off. Confidence: **high**.

### FC-10 — Attestation and proposal signing roots bind no network/genesis identity

- **Severity:** Low today (slashing inert); Medium once `SLASHING_EVIDENCE` arms.
- **Status:** NEW for attestations (the `SIGHASH_NETWORK_BINDING` / `DS_EXIT2` decisions and "one validator key per network" operator rule in `deploy/FLAG-DAY-LIFECYCLE.md` §12 cover spends and exits only).
- **Files:** `attestation.rs:42-52` (`DS_ATTEST ‖ slot ‖ head ‖ source ‖ target`), `header.rs:225-230` (`DS_PROPOSE ‖ header`, no network field in `BlockHeaderV4`).
- **Description.** A key used on a devnet and on mainnet (or across a chain split) produces, by `is_double_vote` (`attestation.rs:65`), a slashable pair for every epoch it attests on both — the roots differ, the target epochs coincide. Cross-network attestations are also accepted into the gossip pool as `Hold` forever-unknown roots (bounded, `PendingDutyLimit`), harmless. The risk is honest operators being slashable by evidence assembled from two networks once evidence is live.
- **Recommendation.** Fold `genesis_root` (or `network_binding()`) into `DS_ATTEST`/`DS_PROPOSE` preimages behind a flag day, or make the slashing evidence validator require both attestations' roots to be on the judging chain. Confidence: **high** on the encoding; **medium** on exploitability (depends on the slashing validator, out of scope).

### FC-11 — The leak is absolute (satoshis) while `duty_roster_at` rescales effective stake; a residual leak can zero a validator the moment a cap binds

- **Severity:** Low (needs a non-trivial residual ledger at the moment the cohort cap — or the gated combined cap — first shrinks weights; post-2700 recovery drains residuals over ~100–500 epochs so the window is bounded, but the ledger has never been measured — post-mortem open item 1).
- **Status:** NEW.
- **Files:** `transition.rs:2532` (cap applied first), `:2569-2576` then `with_leak_applied` subtracts the absolute `leaked_of(index)` (`:5316-5333`, saturating).
- **Description.** A cohort validator with 142 M BLCH effective stake that accrued a 5 M BLCH leak keeps 96 % of its weight; when the cohort cap scales it to ~7 k BLCH (FC-02 arithmetic at bps ≈ 9400 with one 25 k outsider), `7k − 5M` saturates to 0. If every cohort member carries a residual larger than its capped stake, the *entire cohort* drops to zero weight in one boundary and the outsider holds 100 %. Recommendation: store/subtract the leak as a fraction of effective stake (or scale the ledger with the cap), and publish the measured ledger before L. Confidence: **high** on arithmetic, **low** on whether a large residual exists.

### FC-12 — Committed `fc_equivocators` bar is permanent and has no exit/slash/recovery path

- **Severity:** Low. **Status:** KNOWN (`forkchoice.rs:118-122` "permanently"; TM2 "bans permanently"). One accidental equivocation (restart without slashing protection, doppelgänger) permanently deletes 1/64 of fork-choice weight from the chain state with no ejection, no penalty, no way back except a hard fork. With finality weight untouched (finality's discard is per-epoch, `finality.rs:434-444`), the two layers disagree on what an equivocation costs. Recommend tying the bar to the (future) slashing ejection so it ends at `exit_epoch`.

### FC-13 — Arithmetic/panic notes (Info)

- `lib.rs:285` `is_epoch_boundary(slot) = (slot + 1) % 32 == 0` overflows at `u64::MAX` (release profile has `overflow-checks`); only test callers exist (`grep`), so not reachable — but it is `pub` and one node-side use on a peer slot would be a remote panic. Use `slot % 32 == 31`.
- `transition.rs:4937` `closing * SLOTS_PER_EPOCH` unchecked; bounded by `MAX_EPOCH_ADVANCE` per block from committed history, unreachable in practice.
- The only `consensus_invariant!` (`transition.rs:5297`) compares two derivations of `roster_next` (a BTreeMap-keyed registry, deduped by construction); not attacker-reachable. Verified `epoch_committees` on an empty roster returns 32 empty vectors, so the empty case also holds.
- No division by attacker-controlled values found in scope (`sample` guards `total > 0`; `cap_status` guards `bps < 10000`; `is_supermajority` guards `total > 0`; `cohort_share_bps` guards `total == 0`).
- Determinism: all committed collections are `BTreeMap/BTreeSet`; `forkchoice::Store` uses `HashMap` but every consumer is a commutative sum or a max with an explicit tie-break; no clocks (`wall_slot` is node-only, `slot_at` takes time as an argument); no floats on consensus paths.

## 3. Positive observations

- Quorum test is exact integer `3w ≥ 2·total` with `u128` and explicit overflow proofs (`finality.rs:470-486`); the "at most one root justified per epoch per node" argument still holds with the floor (two ≥ 2/3 quorums would sum past `leak_adjusted ≤ total_active`).
- Uniform-source rule, equivocator discard on both sides, `source < target` re-checked at the gadget (`finality.rs:449-458`) even though `attestation::validate`, gossip step 2 and the producer filter all enforce it — four consistent enforcement points, no divergence found. `surrounds` and `is_double_vote` (`attestation.rs:59-67`) match Casper exactly.
- Roster split is closed for real: `epoch_committees` has no stake filter (`committees.rs:399-405`), `with_leak_applied` keeps zeroed records, `votes_from_partition` takes the partition set and the denominator set as two parameters, and the mutation tests (`RESTORE_ZERO_STAKE_FILTER`, `LEAK_DROPS_ZEROED`, `PARTITION_DUPLICATES_AN_INDEX`) are wired to the production functions and thread-local. The R1 M7 fix (slash sets `exit_epoch = epoch+1`) keeps the index set frozen within an epoch; the legacy `Exit` also writes a future `exit_epoch`, so no live path mutates the partition mid-epoch.
- The rehearsal switches are `#[cfg(test)]`, the `consensus_invariant!` macro is cfg-free and tested to fire; `report_boundary_vote_drop` runs in release.
- Fisher-Yates with rejection-reduced 128-bit draws, canonical sort + dedup, sorted committees for `binary_search`; the R1 M8 memo is keyed on (mix, epoch, roster fingerprint, mutation flag) and differentially tested.
- RANDAO: single preimage per commitment, exhaustion checked before the hash, reveal becomes next commitment (no replay), header mix must equal `mix_in(parent, reveal)` (`transition.rs:5590-5596`), recommit signing root binds validator/epoch/c0.
- Gossip: `Reject` is type-separated from `Ignore`; signature before `Hold` (R3 NEW-1), 256-entry FIFO plus 2-per-duty caps, `Unjudgeable`/`UnknownValidator` never penalise; `seen` bounded by the 2-epoch prune; saturating window arithmetic on the peer-supplied slot.
- Transition: `MAX_EPOCH_ADVANCE` bounds the boundary walk before the state clone; `MAX_ATTESTATIONS_PER_BLOCK` and the hoisted single partition draw remove the quadratic per-attestation shuffle; every flag-day gate reads the committed `st.epoch`, never a clock; the header commits to body/attestations/coherence root.
- The 2700 flag day is pinned (`prova::the_quorum_floor_binds_at_epoch_2700`), the incident is reproduced with a cure (`s0_*`), and `finality.rs`'s module header now states the cross-node limit of the safety argument, as the post-mortem asked.
- Attest-then-propose timing (`engine.rs:5212-5217`: attest at slot start, propose at +slot/3) means every slot's committee votes are includable in that slot's own block, including the last slot of an epoch — a hypothesis I checked and dropped.

## 5. Residual risk / not covered

- **Hypotheses checked and dropped:** last-slot committee votes are includable (attest at t=0, propose at t=10 s); `is_epoch_boundary` overflow is not reachable from peers; `epoch_committees` cache is per-thread and value-identical; `consensus_invariant!` at 5297 is not input-reachable; no division-by-zero found; `process_epoch` denominator floor arithmetic is correct; `finalization` cannot move sideways within one fold.
- **Not verified from the tree:** whether the fleet actually crossed 2700 uniformly (no fork) — the runbook's abort criteria are operational; the size of the live leak ledger (post-mortem open item 1) — FC-01/FC-11 severity depends on it only marginally.
- **Out of my scope, flagged for the owners:** the slashing evidence validator (`slashing.rs`, FC-10's second half); `engine.rs` reorg/latch semantics beyond what I read (R3 M-1 override logging, F-03); the mempool `admissible` policy as the only defence for FC-03; `ws.rs` checkpoint provenance (`FinalityState::new` trusts any checkpoint; no caller yet).
- **RANDAO chain exhaustion** (≈ 2027-02-11 for the first validators, `RANDAO_RECOMMIT` inert): drawn-but-exhausted proposers produce empty slots and the mix stops moving — progressive liveness decay and a more predictable beacon; KNOWN (`deploy/FLAG-DAY-LIFECYCLE.md` §1), not re-derived here.
- **Accountable safety is not enforced** (slashing inert): every FFG safety statement in this crate is "no honest node finalizes two conflicting checkpoints from one vote history", not "an attacker who does loses stake". KNOWN (dossier F-4, `slashing_backed_finality_claims.rs`).
