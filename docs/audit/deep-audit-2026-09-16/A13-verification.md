# Annex A13 — Adversarial verification of the High and Medium findings

Method: every High/Medium finding was handed to a *skeptical verifier* (one per area annex) whose brief was to refute it from the code, to check the precondition against the live chain, and to re-judge severity; every High was additionally handed to an *independent second reviewer* who reconstructed the attack from scratch and wrote a reproduction test or plan (Annex A14). Verdicts: CONFIRMED, PARTIALLY_CONFIRMED (mechanism real, scope or severity narrower than claimed), REFUTED, UNVERIFIABLE. Where the two verifiers disagreed the lead decided and says so in the note.

## A13.0 Coverage of this pass

The verification ran as four parallel workflows (33 agents). Returned: skeptic verdicts for A1, A2, A4, A5, A6, A7, A8, A9, A10, A11 (68 findings) and independent second-lens reviews for TX-11, TX-12, INF-01, NET-01 and NET-03. **Not returned** (agent budget exhausted mid-run): the skeptic pass for A3 (ST-01…ST-05), the second lens for TX-01, FC-01, FC-02, FC-03, ST-01, ST-02, EN-01…EN-04, NET-02, CR-01, LG-01, INF-02, BV-01…BV-03, and the completeness critic. For TX-01/FC-03/ST-02, FC-01, EN-01 and INF-05 the lead's own source reading (§A13.5) stands in for the missing second lens; ST-03…ST-05 keep their reviewer severity with the verification column marked pending. The workflow can be resumed from its journals to fill the gaps.

## A13.2 Skeptic evidence per finding

### EN-01 — One admissible transaction censors every transaction on the network (`select_transactions` breaks on the first over-cap entry)
- **Verdict:** CONFIRMED; severity_ok=True; proposed High; live_today=True
- **Evidence:** engine.rs:3210-3213 `let n=(encoded.len() as u64).max(declared); if bytes.saturating_add(n)>cap {break;}` — a break, not continue, over a tip-DESC ordering (3181-3184). admissible V1 Transfer arm (5621-5688) has no encoded/declared vs block-cap check, no outpoint-ownership, no in-tx dup check (those are consensus-only: transition.rs:3932/3941); it verifies each input's sig against the CARRIED pubkey, so a ~530KB/64-input transfer signed with the attacker's own key is admissible. 64 inputs → intrinsic_gas≈13.1M<60M (HYBRID_VERIFY_GAS=72748, fee_market.rs:566); wire ≈533k>V2 cap 524288 (BLOCK_BYTES_V2=800, live). sweep only checks outpoint existence; test 7447 uses tip 0 and never covers a high-tip over-cap entry.
- **Notes:** Real bug (not just design): the highest-tip over-cap tx empties the whole selection on every proposer → network-wide value-transfer censorship, no precondition, sustained by one frame/50min. Blocks and finality continue, so High (not Critical) is correct.

- **Evidence:** MEMPOOL_MAX=4096 (engine.rs:204) is a COUNT; no byte budget exists on the mempool (net.rs ENGINE_QUEUE_BYTES_CAP=64MiB bounds only the queue, released at engine.rs:5335). ### EN-04 — `on_transaction` runs an O(mempool) SHA3 scan *before* any cheap refusal: ~40 ms of consensus-thread CPU per 120-byte frame
- **Verdict:** CONFIRMED; severity_ok=True; proposed High; live_today=True
- **Evidence:** on_transaction runs the per-source SHA3 scan `self.mempool.values().filter(|t| tx_source_hash(t)==Some(source))` (engine.rs:3041-3047) — tx_source_hash (514) does Sha3_256::digest of each entry's first-input pubkey — AFTER only dedup(3024)/is_rejected(3032) and BEFORE capacity(3058) and admissible/verify(3117). No cached source hash or counter. A ~120-byte 1-input transfer triggers the O(mempool) scan then is refused free by admissible, and refused txs are not added to `rejected`, so the same frame is infinitely resendable. On the consensus thread; net.rs inbound has no data-frame rate limit.
- **Notes:** ~10µs/entry (3.75KB pubkey) → ~40ms at 4096 entries, ~5ms at 500. Impact scales with mempool population, which the attacker controls via EN-02 or that accrues organically on observers. Single-node CPU DoS by an unauthenticated peer at KB/s — High.
