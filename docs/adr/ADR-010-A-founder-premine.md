## 1. Context

ADR-010 established the hybrid tokenomics model (tail emission + endowment + boost) based on ~50,000 Monte Carlo trajectories. One critical question remained open: **how to distribute the pre-mining allocation (founder + pool) at genesis?**

Two design constraints from the founder (BLOCH Core) determine the answer:

1. **L2 will be operated with a self-managed pool** — not delegated to third parties or DAO governance. However, this does **not** require the founder allocation to be liquid at day 1; L2 deployment can be funded from operational pool allocations rather than founder personal allocation.
2. **Token must be utility-driven, not store-of-value** — explicit rejection of the Bitcoin model (token with no in-ecosystem use). BLOCH follows the Ethereum philosophy: token is the gas, the staking asset, the payment unit, the governance signal — token velocity is generated by the ecosystem, not by speculation.

These constraints, combined with the founder's stated commitment to a fair-launch ethos and absence of personal short-term profit motive, lead to an **unusually conservative allocation structure**:

- **17% founder allocation** (matching Solana team allocation)
- **30-year linear vesting with 12-month cliff** — 7.5x longer than Solana's 4 years
- **Strong fair-launch narrative** — surpasses Bitcoin (premine 0%), Litecoin (0%), and matches/exceeds Monero in long-term commitment

This ADR formalizes that structure.

## 2. Decision Drivers

- **D1.** Fair-launch credibility: 30-year lock with 17% allocation positions BLOCH among the most conservative founder structures in crypto history.
- **D2.** Howey defense maximization: long-term lock is the strongest available evidence of "founder skin in the game" for SEC and MiCA Article 6 compliance.
- **D3.** Tier-1 exchange listings: Coinbase/Kraken/Binance review processes accept and reward conservative vesting schedules. 30-year lock removes this as a friction point entirely.
- **D4.** Token utility model: BLOCH must be usable within its ecosystem (gas, staking, payments, governance) — not designed primarily as a passive store of value. Tokenomics should support this design.
- **D5.** Founder long-term commitment: 30-year vesting signals 30+ year commitment to the project. Aligns founder incentives with chain longevity.
- **D6.** Tail risk asymmetry: if BLOCH succeeds long-term, locked founder allocation appreciates over decades; if it fails, vesting was irrelevant. Strong upside, limited downside.
- **D7.** Operational separation: L2 deployment, market making, and ecosystem grants do not need to come from founder personal allocation — they come from the validator/oracle pool (separate ADR-012) or future foundation treasury.
- **D8.** Compatibility with ADR-010: emission curve must arithmetically reconcile with premine allocation.

## 3. Considered Options

### 3.1 Option A — 10% founder, 8-year vesting (legacy proposal)

Original proposal in legacy BLOCH memory: 100M founder + 30M pool = 130M genesis premine.

**Pros:** smaller allocation, slightly faster vesting.
**Cons:** "10% with 8-year vesting" is closer to industry standard but not particularly distinctive. Founder still has decision power for years. Doesn't differentiate BLOCH strongly from competitors.

### 3.2 Option B — 17% founder, 4-year vesting (Solana pattern)

Match Solana team allocation (17%) with industry-standard 4-year vesting (cliff 12m + linear 36m).

**Pros:** widely accepted by tier-1 exchanges. Compatible with VC fundraising norms.
**Cons:** does not differentiate BLOCH on fair-launch axis. Founder fully unlocked by year 5 — same trajectory as any other PoS launch.

### 3.3 Option C — 17% founder, 30-year vesting ✅ SELECTED

Match Solana allocation (17%) but with **30-year linear vesting** (cliff 12m + linear 348m = 29 years linear after cliff).

**Pros:**
- Most conservative founder structure of any major chain.
- Maximizes Howey defense and regulatory positioning.
- Strong fair-launch narrative — passes Bitcoin in conservativeness (Bitcoin had premine 0% but Satoshi mined ~1.1M BTC fully liquid from day 1).
- Founder fully unlocked only at year 30 — aligns with "post-quantum era" thesis (PQ threat materializes 10-25 years out).
- Differentiates BLOCH strongly in cripto landscape.

**Cons:**
- Founder personal liquidity is severely constrained. Annual unlock is ~5.7M BLOCH (170M / 30) after cliff — meaningful but not enormous.
- Industry-unusual — may surprise listing reviewers who expect 4-year norm.
- Cannot be undone — 30-year commitment is essentially permanent.

### 3.4 Option D — 0% founder, 100% mining

Pure fair-launch like Bitcoin/Litecoin/Monero. Founder gets nothing pre-mine.

**Pros:** maximum fair-launch credibility.
**Cons:** founder cannot fund operations or be compensated for years of pre-mainnet work. Inviable for a founder who has invested significant time and capital. Also: most "0% premine" chains have founder mining the early blocks anyway, which is functionally equivalent to a small premine without the disclosure.

## 4. Decision Outcome

**Adopt Option C: 17% founder allocation with 30-year linear vesting.**

### 4.1 Premine Allocation Structure

| Allocation | BLOCH | % of supply | Lock structure |
|------------|------|-------------|----------------|
| Founder (locked) | 170,000,000 | 17.0% | Cliff 12 months + linear 348 months |
| Validator/Oracle Pool | 30,000,000 | 3.0% | Operational (ADR-012) |
| **Total premine** | **200,000,000** | **20.0%** | |
| Mining (halvings) | 800,000,000 | 80.0% | Emitted over ~30 years via halving curve |
| **Total nominal supply** | **1,000,000,000** | **100.0%** | |

### 4.2 Founder Wallet Structure

The 170M founder allocation is held in a **single canonical wallet** governed by consensus rules — **not** a smart contract, **not** a multisig. The lock is enforced as a transaction validity rule at the protocol level:

```
INVARIANT: total_outflow_from_founder_wallet(t) <=
           floor( (t - cliff_block) / vesting_blocks_per_unit )
                 × per_block_unlock_amount
```

This makes the lock:
- **Tamper-proof** — cannot be modified except by hardfork
- **Auditable** — any node can verify lock compliance from chain state
- **Trust-minimized** — no smart contract bug surface, no admin keys

### 4.3 Vesting Schedule

```
Total locked:              170,000,000 BLOCH
Cliff duration:            12 months (= 1 halving interval = 210,000 blocks)
Linear duration:           348 months (29 years post-cliff)
Total lock duration:       30 years (12m cliff + 348m linear)

Per-month unlock (post-cliff):  170M / 348 ≈ 488,506 BLOCH/month
Per-block unlock (post-cliff):  ≈ 27.91 BLOCH/block

Cumulative unlock at year 5:    19.5M BLOCH  (11.5% of allocation)
Cumulative unlock at year 10:   53.5M BLOCH  (31.5%)
Cumulative unlock at year 20:   121.5M BLOCH (71.5%)
Cumulative unlock at year 30:   170.0M BLOCH (100%)
```

### 4.4 Rust Implementation

```rust
// src/tokenomics/founder_lock.rs

use crate::tokenomics::types::*;

/// Founder allocation: 17% of total supply.
pub const FOUNDER_ALLOCATION_SATS: u64 = 170_000_000 * 100_000_000;

/// Cliff duration in blocks (12 months at ~150s block time = 210k blocks).
pub const FOUNDER_CLIFF_BLOCKS: u64 = 210_000;

/// Linear vesting duration after cliff (29 years = 348 months = 29 × 210k blocks).
pub const FOUNDER_LINEAR_BLOCKS: u64 = 29 * 210_000;

/// Total lock duration: 30 years.
pub const FOUNDER_TOTAL_LOCK_BLOCKS: u64 =
    FOUNDER_CLIFF_BLOCKS + FOUNDER_LINEAR_BLOCKS;

/// Founder canonical address (set at genesis, immutable).
pub const FOUNDER_GENESIS_ADDRESS: [u8; 32] = [
    // populated from founder-genesis keystore at genesis spec finalization
    0u8; 32
];

/// Compute the maximum cumulative amount that can have been spent
/// from the founder wallet by a given block height.
pub fn founder_max_cumulative_spendable(height: u64) -> u64 {
    if height < FOUNDER_CLIFF_BLOCKS {
        return 0;  // Cliff: nothing unlocked
    }

    let blocks_post_cliff = height - FOUNDER_CLIFF_BLOCKS;

    if blocks_post_cliff >= FOUNDER_LINEAR_BLOCKS {
        return FOUNDER_ALLOCATION_SATS;  // Fully unlocked
    }

    // Linear unlock
    (FOUNDER_ALLOCATION_SATS as u128
        * blocks_post_cliff as u128
        / FOUNDER_LINEAR_BLOCKS as u128) as u64
}

/// Validate that a transaction from the founder wallet does not violate
/// the lock invariant. Called during transaction validation.
pub fn validate_founder_outflow(
    tx: &Transaction,
    chain_state: &ChainState,
) -> Result<(), TokenomicsError> {
    if tx.from != FOUNDER_GENESIS_ADDRESS {
        return Ok(());  // Not a founder tx
    }

    let height = chain_state.current_height();
    let max_spendable = founder_max_cumulative_spendable(height);
    let already_spent = chain_state.founder_total_outflow();
    let proposed_total = already_spent.saturating_add(tx.value);

    if proposed_total > max_spendable {
        return Err(TokenomicsError::FounderLockViolation {
            attempted: proposed_total,
            allowed: max_spendable,
            block_height: height,
        });
    }

    Ok(())
}

#[cfg(test)]
mod tests {
    use super::*;

    #[test]
    fn cliff_locks_everything() {
        assert_eq!(founder_max_cumulative_spendable(0), 0);
        assert_eq!(
            founder_max_cumulative_spendable(FOUNDER_CLIFF_BLOCKS - 1),
            0
        );
    }

    #[test]
    fn cliff_end_starts_linear() {
        // Right at cliff end, 0 should still be spendable (linear=0)
        assert_eq!(
            founder_max_cumulative_spendable(FOUNDER_CLIFF_BLOCKS),
            0
        );
    }

    #[test]
    fn fully_unlocked_at_30_years() {
        assert_eq!(
            founder_max_cumulative_spendable(FOUNDER_TOTAL_LOCK_BLOCKS),
            FOUNDER_ALLOCATION_SATS
        );
    }

    #[test]
    fn unlock_at_year_10_approximately() {
        // Year 10 = 10 × 210k blocks = 2.1M blocks
        // Post-cliff: 2.1M - 210k = 1.89M blocks
        // Fraction: 1.89M / (29 × 210k) = 1.89M / 6.09M ≈ 0.310
        // Expected: ~31% of 170M = ~52.7M BLOCH
        let height = 10 * 210_000;
        let unlocked = founder_max_cumulative_spendable(height);
        let expected_sats = (170_000_000u64 * 100_000_000u64) * 31 / 100;
        let tolerance = 5_000_000u64 * 100_000_000u64;  // ±5M BLOCH
        assert!(
            (unlocked as i64 - expected_sats as i64).abs() < tolerance as i64
        );
    }
}
```

### 4.6 Validator/Oracle Pool (30M)

Pool allocation of 30M BLOCH is operational and managed by the founder during the bootstrap phase. Detailed structure (sub-wallets, governance transition plan, etc.) deferred to **ADR-012**, which will specify:

- Sub-allocation by purpose (validator incentives, oracle rewards, bug bounties, ecosystem grants)
- Disclosure schedule
- Future governance transition (foundation establishment in years 2-5)

Pool is **not subject to the same 30-year lock as founder personal**, because it is operational capital required for chain bootstrap (validator incentives, market making liquidity, exchange listings). Pool wallet movements will be publicly disclosed quarterly.

## 5. Consequences

### 5.1 Positive

- **Fair-launch narrative is exceptionally strong.** 30-year lock surpasses every major chain in conservative founder commitment.
- **Howey defense at maximum.** SEC counsel has strongest possible argument: founder is locked into the chain's success for 30 years, eliminating "rug pull" concern entirely.
- **Tier-1 listings become trivial on the vesting axis.** Coinbase/Kraken/Binance reviewers will see the 30-year schedule and immediately move on — non-issue.
- **Founder long-term alignment with chain success.** No incentive to short-term dump; full alignment with multi-decade thesis.
- **Differentiation in market positioning.** "BLOCH — the post-quantum chain with the most conservative founder lock in crypto history" is a defensible, distinctive narrative.
- **Compatible with ETH-style utility token model.** Founder allocation does not interfere with token velocity; ecosystem demand drives utility, not founder distribution.

### 5.2 Negative

- **Severe founder personal liquidity constraint.** Annual unlock of ~5.7M BLOCH post-cliff is meaningful only if BLOCH appreciates significantly. In bear scenarios, founder receives little real compensation for decades of work.
- **Cannot be reversed.** A 30-year commitment is functionally permanent. If circumstances change, founder is locked in.
- **Industry-unusual schedule may confuse some stakeholders.** Investors familiar with 4-year norms may misinterpret 30-year as suspicious. Mitigation: clear documentation and proactive narrative.
- **Pool (30M) and founder (170M) operate under different rules.** Requires careful documentation to avoid confusion — founder personal is locked, operational pool is not.
- **No tax flexibility for founder.** Taxes on unlock events occur at unlock time; cannot be optimized via early sale strategies.

### 5.3 Open Risks

- **R1.** Founder operational needs may exceed unlock schedule in early years. If founder needs BLOCH liquidity for personal reasons (medical, family, etc.) and cannot access locked allocation, no mechanism exists. Mitigation: founder personal financial planning must accommodate this constraint pre-mainnet.
- **R2.** Hardfork to modify founder lock would be reputationally catastrophic. Even if technically possible, attempting to shorten the lock would destroy fair-launch credibility. This is a feature, not a bug — but it must be understood as final.
- **R3.** Founder death or incapacity scenarios. Inheritance/succession plan should be documented separately. Lock continues regardless of founder status; designated successor inherits the same vesting schedule.

## 6. ETH-Style Utility Token Model — Design Philosophy

This ADR commits BLOCH to the **utility token model** (Ethereum-style), explicitly rejecting the **store-of-value model** (Bitcoin-style). Implications:

### 6.1 Token must be USED within the ecosystem

Beyond holding, BLOCH must serve concrete functions:
- **Gas** — every transaction pays fees in BLOCH
- **Staking** — validators bond BLOCH for FFG participation
- **Governance** — protocol parameter votes weighted by BLOCH
- **Payment unit** — services within ecosystem priced in BLOCH
- **Collateral** — DeFi primitives accept BLOCH as collateral
- **Bridge fuel** — L2 operations consume BLOCH

### 6.2 Velocity > Hoarding

A successful utility token has **high velocity** — it changes hands frequently as users transact. This contrasts with store-of-value tokens where holders aggregate and rarely transact.

Implication for tokenomics: tail emission and endowment buffer should not be designed to prop up speculative price; they should sustain operational security so the ecosystem can function reliably.

### 6.3 Demand follows utility, not narrative

Bitcoin's value comes from collective belief in scarcity. Ethereum's value comes from collective use of the network. BLOCH targets the latter:

- **More dApps deployed** → more BLOCH needed for gas
- **More RWA tokenized** → more BLOCH needed for collateral and fees
- **More L2 operations** → more BLOCH burned/consumed
- **More validators** → more BLOCH staked

This is captured in a separate document: **`BLOCH_Ecosystem_Products.md`** — concrete product lines that drive token velocity. See companion document.
